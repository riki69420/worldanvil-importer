ELARIS IMPORTER — HOW TO USE IT
================================

This turns your Fantasia Archive export and your Azgaar map into World Anvil
articles and a map image. Read this top to bottom once; it takes two minutes.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
1. Your Fantasia Archive export. That is the .zip file you downloaded from
   Fantasia Archive (for example "Elaris - Export.zip"). You do NOT need to
   unzip it.

2. Your Azgaar map file (the one ending in .map). Optional — skip it if you
   only want the articles.

3. Windows 10 or 11 with Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome installed. Edge is
   already on every Windows PC; it is used to draw the map image.


GETTING THE PROGRAM
-------------------
1. Go to:  https://github.com/riki69420/claude/releases
2. Under the newest release, click  ElarisImporter-v1.0.2.zip  (or whatever
   the newest version number is). It downloads.
3. Unzip it anywhere, for example your Desktop. Inside:

     ElarisImporter.exe     the program
     INSTRUCTIONS.txt       this file
     extension\             the Chrome/Edge helper (Step 2C)

The download contains no worldbuilding data — only the tools. Your export
and map stay on your own computer; nothing is uploaded anywhere by Step 1.

First time you run the exe Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". That
is because the program is not signed with a paid certificate, nothing more.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway".


STEP 1 — CONVERT (no World Anvil account needed for this)
---------------------------------------------------------
1. Open ElarisImporter.exe
2. Next to "Fantasia Archive export", click Browse… and pick your export .zip
3. Next to "Azgaar map", click Browse… and pick your .map file (or leave empty)
4. "Output folder" is where the results go. The default is fine.
5. Click  [ Convert ]
6. Wait for the log at the bottom to say "Done."
7. Click  [ Open output folder ]

WHAT YOU GET IN THE OUTPUT FOLDER
   bbcode\             One text file per article, sorted by World Anvil type
                       (settlement, location, person, species, …). Each file
                       is ready to paste into World Anvil.
   map\elaris.png      Your map as a big image, ready to upload as a
                       World Anvil map.
   map\burgs.csv       Every town on the map with its position, for placing
                       map pins.
   dangling-links.txt  Links in your articles that point at something that
                       does not exist in your export. Usually a typo in
                       Fantasia Archive. Fix them there and convert again,
                       or ignore them.


STEP 2A — PUT ARTICLES INTO WORLD ANVIL BY HAND
-----------------------------------------------
Works for everyone, no subscription needed. Slow but simple.

For each file in the bbcode folder:
1. In World Anvil, click "Create article" and choose the type that matches
   the folder name (a file in bbcode\settlement is a Settlement, and so on).
2. Open the text file. Copy the TITLE line into the article title.
3. Copy everything below "--- CONTENT ---" into the big text box.
4. If the file has a "--- SIDEBAR ---" part, paste that into the sidebar box.
5. Save.

The @[Name] bits in the text turn into links automatically once the article
with that name exists. Do the locations first, then everything else, and the
links resolve themselves.


STEP 2B — UPLOAD AUTOMATICALLY (needs a World Anvil Guild membership)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
World Anvil only lets you upload by program if you have a Worldbuilder's
Guild rank ABOVE Grandmaster and have applied for an "application key".
If you do not have that, skip this and use 2A.

If you do:
1. Get your two keys:
     Application key — from the World Anvil developer application you
                       were approved for.
     Auth token      — World Anvil → your profile → API Keys (the
                       "Get tokens…" button in the app opens that page).
2. Paste both into the app.
3. Click  [ Load worlds ]  and pick your world from the list.
4. Click  [ Convert and upload ]
5. Watch the log. Each article says "created" as it goes.

Safe to run again: it remembers what it already created and updates those
instead of making duplicates. Articles are created as PRIVATE so nothing is
public until you decide.

"Remember credentials on this computer" saves your keys so you do not have to
paste them next time. They are saved as plain text on your PC — leave it
unticked on a shared computer.


STEP 2C — THE CHROME EXTENSION (faster than 2A, no subscription needed)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
This is a helper for doing it by hand. It fills the article editor for you,
one article per click. World Anvil's editor saves by itself a moment later
(the little clock at the bottom of the editor updates).

Install it once:
1. Use the "extension" folder from the zip you downloaded.
2. In Chrome or Edge open:  chrome://extensions   (edge://extensions)
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and pick the "extension" folder.
5. Click the new puzzle-piece icon → Elaris Paste Assistant → "Load
   articles.json…" and pick the articles.json from your output folder.

THE FAST WAY — "Fill all shown"
1. Open any one article of your world in World Anvil's editor (so the panel
   knows which world). Bettiea Lundereth will do.
2. In the panel, leave the dropdown on "All types" and press
   [Fill all shown (126)]. It asks you to click once more to confirm.
3. Wait. It creates every article first (a name flashes by every second),
   then writes the text into each one. About 5 minutes for 126 articles.
   [Stop] halts after the current article; pressing Fill all again carries on
   where it left off.
4. Reload the page. Every article is in World Anvil's tree, with the links
   between them already working, title, text, sidebar and excerpt filled.

   An article that already exists with the same name and has text in it is
   left alone and just ticked, so nothing you wrote by hand is overwritten.
   An article that exists but is empty is used rather than duplicated.

   Only names that exist in neither World Anvil nor your export stay as plain
   text (they are listed in dangling-links.txt from Step 1).

TEMPLATE BOXES
   Fill all also fills World Anvil's own boxes where Fantasia Archive has
   the same thing: population and size for places, sex/age/height/weight
   and species for characters, lifespan for species, price for currencies,
   and so on. The same information also stays in the main text under its
   heading, so nothing is lost if a box has no match.

FOLDERS
   Fill all also files each article into a folder by kind (Characters, Items,
   Currencies, Settlements, Geography, Buildings & Landmarks, Species,
   Languages, Professions & Classes, Spells & Skills, Organizations,
   Religions, Schools of Magic, Materials), making the folder if needed.
   [Sort into folders] does the same for articles that were already on
   World Anvil before (it leaves anything that is already in a folder where
   it is). Reload the page to see the tree; drag things around afterwards as
   usual.

STARTING OVER
   [Delete imported…] at the bottom of the panel removes every article in
   the loaded list from World Anvil, plus the folders this tool made, and
   clears the ticks. It asks twice and tells you if any of those articles
   were not made by the tool (same name, written by hand) — those go too.
   There is no undo from here, so read the red button before clicking it a
   second time. Afterwards reload the page and press Fill all shown again.

THE SLOW WAY — one article at a time (repeat once per article):
1. On World Anvil, press "+ Article" (bottom left). Pick the tile whose name
   matches the badge next to the article in the panel (Character, Geography,
   Settlement, Item, …). An empty editor opens.
2. In the panel on the right, find the article and click  [Fill].
   The panel looks up the links first ("Looking up links… 3/9"), then fills
   the title, the main text and the sidebar. Takes a few seconds.
3. Read it over. Wait for the clock at the bottom of the editor to update —
   that is World Anvil saving. Then press "+ Article" for the next one.
   Filled articles are ticked and sink to the bottom of the list.

Tip: use the "All types" dropdown in the panel to do all Characters, then
all Items, and so on — fewer trips through the template picker.

ABOUT LINKS  (@[Name] in your text)
   A link can only be made to an article that already exists on World Anvil.
   When [Fill] meets a name that does not exist yet, it writes the name as
   plain text and shows a yellow "3 links" badge on that article. Once all
   your articles exist, choose "Links to redo" in the dropdown, open each of
   those articles again (click it in World Anvil's tree) and press [Fill]
   again: everything is rewritten with the links in place. Doing the
   locations first means fewer articles need this second pass.

   Fill REPLACES the title, main text and sidebar every time. Do not hand-edit
   an article until you are done pressing Fill on it.

Excerpt: World Anvil's new editor has no excerpt box, so the excerpt is not
filled. Click an article's name in the panel to copy it (and the tags and
template fields) for pasting by hand if you want them.

If [Fill] says "partly filled":
   World Anvil's boxes were not recognised (this can happen if World Anvil
   changes its editor, or in the old-style editor). At the bottom of the
   panel click "Title", then click the title box on the page. Do the same for
   "Content". The panel remembers them from then on. [Copy] always works as
   a fallback — it puts the content on the clipboard for Ctrl+V.


THE MAP
-------
map\elaris.png is your map drawn from the .map file. If it looks plain
(no colours, no mountains), that is because those layers were switched off
in Azgaar when you saved the file. Open your map in Azgaar, turn on the layers
you want (Layers menu: biomes, relief, states…), Save again, and convert
again.

To put it on World Anvil: World Anvil → Maps → New map → upload elaris.png.
Then use map\burgs.csv to see where each town goes.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "no Fantasia Archive export found"   You picked the wrong file. Pick the
                                       export .zip, or the folder that has
                                       "Characters", "Items" etc. inside.
- "PNG render skipped"                 No Edge or Chrome was found. Install
                                       one, or open map\elaris.svg in any
                                       browser and take a screenshot.
- Upload says FAILED with 401          Wrong application key or auth token.
- Upload says FAILED with 422          World Anvil rejected a field. Untick
                                       "Send template fields" and try again.
- Anything else                        Copy the text from the log box and
                                       send it along with what you clicked.
